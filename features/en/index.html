How Echo works

See how Echo works,
screen by screen.

Not just a list of feature names. Real screens from the app, walked through step by step so you know exactly how to start and how to use each one.

Getting started

Starting a workout

Pick a sport and tap one button—tracking starts immediately.

  1. 1
    Choose your sportRunning, cycling, walking, hiking or trail running.
  2. 2
    Check your connectionSee your Apple Watch connection status in the top right.
  3. 3
    Tap startPace, heart rate and route recording begin right away.

Live tracking

What you see mid-workout

Just the map and the numbers that matter—everything else stays out of the way.

  1. 1
    Your route draws in real timeThe 3D map fills in as you move.
  2. 2
    Check your current pacePace and Apple Watch heart rate sit together at the bottom.
  3. 3
    Pause or finishPause anytime, or finish and it's saved to your history instantly.

Today, at a glance

Check in from the home screen

You don't need to start a workout to know where today stands.

  1. 1
    Start with the weatherLocal forecast and a suggested workout intensity.
  2. 2
    Read your activity ringSteps, active energy and workout count in one glance.
  3. 3
    Review this weekDistance, count, time and an Echo insight to close out the day.

History and insight

Find change in your record

The same data, viewed three ways: summary, statistics and report.

  1. 1
    Switch tabsSummary at a glance, statistics as trends, reports by month.
  2. 2
    Filter by sportSee everything, or just running, cycling or walking.
  3. 3
    Tap a workoutRoute, splits, elevation and personal-record badges.

Goals and challenges

You set the goal

No leaderboards—challenges are built from your own past records.

  1. 1
    Set this week's goalChoose count, duration, distance or steps, then set the target.
  2. 2
    See challenges made for youCards like "beat your last week" are suggested from your own history.
  3. 3
    Watch the progress bar fillEvery card updates as you move.

Apple Watch

Leave your phone at home

Start and track a full workout with just your Apple Watch.

  1. 1
    Start right from your wristPick a sport and GPS tracking begins standalone on the Watch.
  2. 2
    Check pace, distance and timeAll on the Watch screen, no phone required.
  3. 3
    Syncs automaticallyYour workout appears in the iPhone app the moment you finish.
