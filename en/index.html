01 — The echo

You race yesterday, not everyone else

The name is the product. Your most recent workout in the same sport echoes back and runs alongside your current pace. The only benchmark is you.

There is no leaderboard

No likes, no followers, no public ranking. What you leave behind is a record, and what you come back to is your own rhythm.

02 — Features

Focus on your movement,
not somebody else's feed

Everything you need on screen, and nothing you don't.

GPS workout tracking

Route, distance, duration, pace or speed and estimated calories for runs, walks and rides.

An echo of yesterday

Compare your live pace against your most recent workout in the same sport.

Weather and insight

Local forecast, monthly statistics, personal records and challenge progress in one place.

Goals and challenges

Set your own weekly goal and keep the streak with challenges built from your own history.

Runs on Apple Watch alone

Leave the phone at home. Live Activities on the lock screen and a home screen widget included.

Data you opt into

Detailed workouts stay on your device. Analytics, diagnostics and notifications are each opt-in.

03 — Principles

Built on four principles

01

Private by default

Routes and health records stay on your device unless you choose otherwise.

02

Quiet performance

Reflection over ranking. No likes, no comparison to strangers.

03

Continuous fitness

Starting with running and extending to hiking, swimming and strength.

04

Clear ownership

Export to GPX and take your history with you.

04 — Sports

One record, every movement

05 — Privacy

Routes are recorded only
when you start a workout

Echo does not log your everyday movement. Location is used only to fetch the forecast for where you are, or once you have started a workout.

Echo does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. Weather, GPS, pace, speed and calorie figures are indicative and may differ from actual values.
