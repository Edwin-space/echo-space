Routes are recorded only when you start a workout

Echo does not record your everyday route. Location is used for local weather or after you deliberately begin workout tracking.

Echo does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or emergency services. Weather, GPS, pace, speed and calorie information is for general reference and may differ from actual conditions.
